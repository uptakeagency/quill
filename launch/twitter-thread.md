# Twitter/X Thread

## Tweet 1 (Hook)

AI writes your code. You nod along. But what does half of it actually mean?

I built Quill — a system-wide tech dictionary for macOS.

Select any term. Press Ctrl+Option+Q. Get an instant explanation.

No tabs. No context switch. No copy-pasting into ChatGPT.

Free + open source.

[IMAGE: assets/demo.gif]

## Tweet 2 (Demo)

Here's how it works:

1. Select a term in ANY app — VS Code, Terminal, Safari, anything
2. Press the hotkey
3. A floating panel appears near your cursor
4. It doesn't steal focus from your current app

The panel shows a TL;DR, full explanation, and links to resources.

[IMAGE: assets/ELI5.png]

## Tweet 3 (Key differentiator)

The killer feature: drill-down navigation.

Related terms are highlighted as clickable links. Click one, go deeper. Breadcrumbs let you navigate back.

It's like a Wikipedia rabbit hole for tech concepts.

Plus 5 explanation levels — from ELI5 (simple analogies) to Pro (trade-offs and edge cases).

[IMAGE: assets/resources.png]

## Tweet 4 (Tech stack)

Built with:

- Swift, no Xcode needed
- Hexagonal Architecture (Ports & Adapters)
- macOS Accessibility API for system-wide text capture
- NSPanel with non-activating style (keeps source app focused)
- Protocol-based AI backends (Gemini, Claude — easy to add more)
- ~3K lines of code, 3 dependencies
